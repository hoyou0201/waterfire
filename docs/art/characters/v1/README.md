# WaterFire character concepts v1

Created: 2026-10-21 (Asia/Seoul)
Tool: built-in image_gen (not CLI).
Scope: first visual concepts only; not wired into the game renderer and not animation sheets.

## Design intent

A matching pair of original friendly elemental spirits for the dark Canvas 2D cooperative platformer. Shared eyes, contour treatment, hands and feet; fire uses three flame tips and warm orange/yellow, water uses a single flowing droplet crest and blue/aqua. Transparent backgrounds. These are concept-resolution illustrations; small-scale readability, animation and collision alignment must be checked when integrating.

- `fire-v1.png`: fire character concept.
- `water-v1.png`: water character concept, generated using the fire concept as a style reference.

## Fire prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original fire protagonist concept / transparent 2D platformer character cutout for the WaterFire browser co-op game. Create ONE complete full-body character only, centered on a genuinely transparent background. Design language: charming professional indie 2D game art, bold readable shapes, clean dark charcoal outline, restrained flat cel shading, matte surface, no painterly detail, no 3D. A tiny upright living ember spirit with an elongated rounded body, three asymmetric flame tongues integrated into the top of its head, the tallest tongue swept slightly to the right; tiny mitten-like arms close to the body, two small rounded dark ember feet on the same baseline. Overall silhouette taller than wide, approximately 3 units wide to 5 units tall including flame; compact limbs fit close to the body so it will read at a 24x40 pixel collision size. Warm vermilion-orange body (#ff6b3d), golden yellow inner face/belly patch, dark burnt-orange shadow, dark charcoal outlines; large simple ivory oval eyes with very dark pupils and small friendly confident smile. Facial expression upbeat and brave, approachable, gender-neutral. View: almost straight-on with a subtle three-quarter orientation toward the right, neutral standing idle pose, both eyes and both feet clearly visible. Crisp intentional contour, few large color regions, excellent readability on a dark charcoal stone platformer background. Original character, no resemblance to Fireboy and Watergirl or existing branded mascots. Character fills about 75 percent of canvas height, generous clear margins. No other characters, no sheet, no typography, no watermarks, no scene, no ground plane, no detached fire particles, no cast shadow, no outer glow, no equipment or clothing. Preserve real transparent alpha. This is the FIRE half of a matching fire-and-water duo.

## Water prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original water protagonist concept / transparent 2D platformer character cutout for the WaterFire browser co-op game. Create ONE complete full-body character only, centered on a genuinely transparent background. Design language: charming professional indie 2D game art, bold readable shapes, clean dark charcoal outline, restrained flat cel shading, matte surface, no painterly detail, no 3D. A tiny upright living water droplet spirit with an elongated rounded body, a SINGLE elegant rounded curling droplet crest integrated into the top of its head, curving slightly to the right; tiny mitten-like arms close to the body, two small rounded deep-blue feet on the same baseline. Overall silhouette taller than wide, approximately 3 units wide to 5 units tall including crest; compact limbs fit close to the body so it will read at a 24x40 pixel collision size. Bright sky-blue body (#3da5ff), pale aqua inner face/belly patch, deep blue shadow, dark charcoal outlines; large simple ivory oval eyes with very dark pupils and a small calm friendly smile. Facial expression gentle, clever and quietly playful, approachable, gender-neutral. View: almost straight-on with a subtle three-quarter orientation toward the right, neutral standing idle pose, both eyes and both feet clearly visible. Crisp intentional contour, few large color regions, excellent readability on a dark charcoal stone platformer background. Original character, no resemblance to Fireboy and Watergirl or existing branded mascots. Character fills about 75 percent of canvas height, generous clear margins. No other characters, no sheet, no typography, no watermarks, no scene, no ground plane, no detached bubbles or water droplets, no cast shadow, no outer glow, no equipment or clothing. Preserve real transparent alpha. This is the WATER half of a matching fire-and-water duo. Input image role: STYLE REFERENCE ONLY, showing the fire teammate. Match its illustration style, contour thickness, eye design, cute head/body proportions, feet shape and rendering finish so the water character belongs to exactly the same game. Create the new WATER character as described, not a recolor of the flame: replace all sharp flame tongues with a smooth single droplet-shaped crest, flowing round watery silhouette, and a broad pale-aqua face patch. The final image must contain only the water character.
